## 🧬 Natural Origins

The Aegean is considered one of the oldest natural cat breeds in the world. It developed organically — without human-controlled breeding — on the Greek islands of the Cyclades. As a result, the Aegean has a hardy, adaptable temperament and a strong connection to nature.

These cats are often seen lounging around fishing villages and harbor towns, forming bonds with locals and travelers alike. Despite their rarity as a *formal breed*, they are culturally iconic in Greece.

---

## 🐾 Personality & Behavior

Aegeans are known for their:

- 💬 **Vocal nature** – They love to "talk" to their humans
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Sociability** – Great with families, children, and other pets
- 🧠 **Intelligence** – Quick learners, especially when stimulated
- 🏃 **Playfulness** – Thrive in active households with plenty of toys or outdoor space

They are indoor/outdoor cats by tradition but adjust well to indoor life — especially if they have windows or a perch to observe the outside world.

---

## 🛠️ Care & Maintenance

- **Grooming:** Their semi-long coat is easy to maintain — weekly brushing is usually enough.
- **Diet:** Balanced, protein-rich diet to support their active metabolism.
- **Health:** Generally healthy, thanks to their natural evolution; always ensure vaccinations and parasite prevention are up to date.
