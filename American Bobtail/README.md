## 🧬 Breed History

The American Bobtail is a relatively recent breed, first appearing in the U.S. during the 1960s. Its signature feature — a naturally short "bobbed" tail — is the result of a spontaneous genetic mutation. Despite their wild, lynx-like appearance, these cats are affectionate, loyal, and highly adaptable to family life.

---

## 🐾 Personality & Behavior

The American Bobtail is often described as the "golden retriever of the cat world" for its affectionate and friendly personality. Known for their:

- 🧠 **High intelligence** – They can learn tricks, play fetch, and solve puzzles  
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Strong bonding** – Form deep emotional connections with their humans  
- 🧸 **Gentle playfulness** – Ideal for homes with children or other pets  
- ✈️ **Adaptability** – Calm during travel and change, making them great companions for adventurers

These cats are not overly vocal but will communicate with expressive chirps and body language.

---

## 🛠️ Care & Maintenance

- **Grooming:** Weekly brushing helps manage their thick, shaggy coat
- **Exercise:** They thrive with interactive toys and playtime
- **Health:** A generally robust breed, though responsible breeding is important due to the natural tail mutation

---

## 🔍 Unique Traits

- 🐾 Their tails are usually **1/3 the length** of a normal cat’s, varying from a small nub to a slight curve
- 🐺 Their **wild appearance** is contrasted by a sweet, easy-going nature
- 🧳 Known for handling travel well — even used in **therapy animal programs**
