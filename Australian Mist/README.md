## 🌏 An Aussie Original

Developed in Australia in the 1970s, the Australian Mist is a cross between Burmese, Abyssinian, and domestic short-haired cats. It was specifically bred to be **indoor-friendly, tolerant, and people-loving** — making it one of the most sociable and low-fuss breeds around.

Its beautiful coat pattern (speckled or marbled misting) gives the breed its name and unique look, while its gentle nature makes it **ideal for family life**.

---

## 🐾 Personality & Behavior

Australian Mists are celebrated for their:

- 👶 **Child-friendliness** – Incredibly tolerant and patient, even with young children  
- 🧸 **Cuddle-ready calm** – Equally happy playing or curling up on your lap  
- 🧠 **Bright & interactive nature** – Loves toys, games, and gentle attention  
- 🐾 **Adaptability** – Thrives in homes big or small, and adjusts quickly to new people  
- 🏡 **Indoor suitability** – Bred for indoor life, with low roaming instincts  

They are often described as “dog-like” in their loyalty and desire to be near their humans.

---

## 🛠️ Care & Maintenance

- **Grooming:** Minimal — their short coat rarely sheds heavily  
- **Lifestyle:** Indoor-friendly, making them great apartment companions  
- **Health:** A generally robust breed with no known breed-specific issues  

---

## 🔍 Fun Fact

The Australian Mist was the **first cat breed developed entirely in Australia**, and remains one of the few breeds specifically designed for **indoor companionship**.
