## 🧬 Unique Origins

The American Curl originated in California in the early 1980s, when a stray kitten with unusually curled ears was adopted by a family and began the foundation of the breed. The curled ears result from a **natural genetic mutation** and have become the breed's trademark.

They are still considered a relatively rare breed but are gaining popularity due to their sweet nature and distinct look.

---

## 🐾 Personality & Traits

American Curls are known for their:

- 💞 **Affectionate nature** – Loyal and loving, they enjoy human attention
- 🧠 **Intelligence** – Quick learners, curious explorers
- 👶 **Family-friendly vibe** – Easily adapt to children and other pets
- 🚪 **Social energy** – Will often greet guests and follow people from room to room
- 😺 **Playful spirit** – Stay kitten-like well into adulthood

They adjust easily to new environments and tend to form strong bonds with their families.

---

## 🛠️ Care & Maintenance

- **Grooming:** Low-maintenance; a quick weekly brush is enough
- **Ear Care:** Regular ear checks are important due to their shape
- **Exercise:** Benefit from interactive toys and climbing spaces

---

## 🔍 Fun Fact

Kittens are born with **straight ears** — they begin curling within 2 to 7 days. By 4 months, the curl is set for life!
