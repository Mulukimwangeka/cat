## 🐱 About the Abyssinian

The Abyssinian is one of the oldest known cat breeds, admired for its elegant, ticked coat and lively demeanor. They are incredibly curious, affectionate, and intelligent cats who enjoy interaction and exploration.

Abyssinians are known for being social with both humans and other pets. Their high energy levels and playful nature make them ideal for active households.
